Governed social post search template

Contract verified; deployment blocked on approved credentials, budget, and data-governance scope.

The reviewed v2 contract supports bearer-authenticated recent search, 10–100 results per page, opaque continuation tokens, published rate limits, and credit-based billing. Do not activate a template until an approved developer application, spending cap, query/operator entitlement, retention policy, permitted fields, deletion handling, and provenance requirements are recorded.

When approved, use the generic REST connector with an allow-listed HTTPS endpoint, one explicitly bounded page per call, and no write or stream-rule administration. Never store bearer tokens, response bodies, or personal-data samples in evidence.
