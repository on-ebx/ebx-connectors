Couchbase

Bounded catalog discovery and governed key-value document operations.

1. Deliberate scope

The connector exposes bounded bucket, scope, and collection discovery plus KV get, upsert, and remove. SQL++ queries and paging, DCP, eventing, and CDC are explicitly excluded.

2. Configure

SettingGuidance
Connection stringcouchbases://cluster.example; plaintext couchbase:// is rejected.
CredentialsDedicated least-privilege username/password.
DependencyPinned Java 8-compatible com.couchbase.client:java-client:3.6.2 plus its matching core dependency.

3. Operations

buckets and collections return at most 1000 items. get, upsert, and remove address one document by bucket, scope, collection, and ID. Upsert payloads are limited to 1 MiB by the connector.
