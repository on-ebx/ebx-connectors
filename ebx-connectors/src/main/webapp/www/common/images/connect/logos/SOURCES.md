# Connector logo sources

Connector marks are used only to identify the external system represented by a connector. They do
not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership. Product names and marks remain the property of
their respective owners.

## Vector assets

- `DnB.svg`: Dun & Bradstreet public Brand Center, full-color RGB wordmark,
  <https://www.dnb.com/en-us/about/brand.html>.
- `Gemini.svg`, `GoogleGeocoding.svg`, `Mistral.svg`, and `Ollama.svg`: Simple Icons
  16.31.0. The upstream metadata identifies the corresponding vendor or project source. Simple
  Icons is licensed under CC0-1.0; individual marks remain subject to their owners' trademark
  policies: <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons>.

## Neutral connector symbols

`JDBC.svg`, `JMS.svg`, `SFTP.svg`, `Webhook.svg`, `Countries.svg`, and `default.svg`
identify protocols or generic endpoints rather than a single vendor. They intentionally remain
neutral symbols. `OpenFDA.svg` also remains a neutral openFDA connector mark because FDA restricts
private-sector use of the FDA agency logo.
